CBC / Hematology

heme
Test Typical adult range Why it matters
WBC 4,000-10,000 cells/mcL Infection, inflammation, immune suppression
RBC F: 4.0-5.4 million/mcL
M: 4.5-6.1 million/mcL		 Oxygen-carrying capacity
Hemoglobin F: 11.5-15.5 g/dL
M: 13-17 g/dL		 Anemia, oxygen transport
Hematocrit F: 36-48%
M: 40-55%		 Red cell volume, anemia, dehydration
Platelets 150,000-400,000/mcL Bleeding risk, clotting, DIC monitoring
MCV 80-100 fL Microcytic vs. macrocytic anemia patterns
RDW 12-15% Variation in red cell size

Coagulation

clotting
Test Typical adult range Why it matters
PT 11-13.5 sec Extrinsic/common pathway, warfarin monitoring
INR 0.8-1.1 without anticoagulation
Often 2-3 on warfarin		 Anticoagulation intensity
aPTT 25-35 sec Intrinsic/common pathway, heparin monitoring
Fibrinogen 200-400 mg/dL Low in consumptive coagulopathy such as DIC
D-dimer Usually < 0.50 mcg/mL FEU Clot breakdown; nonspecific when elevated

Electrolytes / Renal

renal
Test Typical adult range Why it matters
Sodium 135-145 mEq/L Fluid balance, neurologic status
Potassium 3.5-5.0 mEq/L Cardiac conduction, ACE/ARB and diuretic safety
Chloride 96-106 mEq/L Fluid and acid-base balance
CO2 / serum bicarbonate 23-29 mEq/L Metabolic acid-base status
Calcium, total 8.5-10.5 mg/dL Neuromuscular function, tetany, lethargy
Magnesium 1.7-2.2 mg/dL Neuromuscular function, rhythm stability
Phosphorus 2.5-4.5 mg/dL Bone, renal, and cellular energy balance
BUN 6-20 mg/dL Renal clearance, hydration, protein breakdown
Creatinine 0.6-1.3 mg/dL Renal filtration; compare to baseline
eGFR ≥ 90 mL/min/1.73 m² CKD staging and kidney function trend

ABGs / Oxygenation

pulmonary
Measure Typical adult range Why it matters
pH 7.35-7.45 Acidosis vs. alkalosis
PaCO2 35-45 mm Hg Ventilation; respiratory acid-base component
HCO3 22-26 mEq/L Metabolic acid-base component
PaO2 75-100 mm Hg on room air Arterial oxygenation
SaO2 / SpO2 95-100% Oxygen saturation; interpret with clinical status

Glucose / Endocrine

endocrine
Test Typical adult range Why it matters
Fasting glucose 70-99 mg/dL Diabetes screening and hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia
Random glucose No fixed normal; diabetes threshold ≥ 200 mg/dL with symptoms Random glucose is diagnostic only with symptoms/context
Hemoglobin A1c < 5.7% Average glycemia over about 3 months
TSH 0.4-4.0 mIU/L Thyroid feedback marker
Free T4 0.8-1.8 ng/dL Circulating thyroid hormone level

Cardiac / Lipids

cardio
Measure Typical adult range Why it matters
Troponin I Usually < 0.04 ng/mL Myocardial injury; assay-specific
BNP Usually < 100 pg/mL Heart failure screening/supporting data
Total cholesterol < 200 mg/dL desirable Atherosclerotic cardiovascular risk
LDL < 100 mg/dL often optimal Primary lipid treatment target
HDL ≥ 60 mg/dL protective
Low: < 40 M, < 50 F		 Higher HDL is generally protective
Triglycerides < 150 mg/dL Cardiometabolic risk

Vitals / Assessment Anchors

assessment
Measure Typical adult range Why it matters
Heart rate 60-100 beats/min Beta blockers, digoxin, shock, dysrhythmias
Respiratory rate 12-20 breaths/min Ventilation, distress, medication assessment
Blood pressure 90/60 to < 120/80 mm Hg Hypertension, shock, medication safety
Temperature About 36.5-37.2°C Fever, infection, inflammatory response
Oxygen saturation 95-100% Oxygenation and respiratory compromise
Urine output At least 0.5 mL/kg/hr Renal perfusion and fluid status

CKD
Stage eGFR Study cue

Stage 2 60-89 Mild decrease

Stage 3b 30-44 Moderate-severe decrease
Stage 4 15-29 Severe decrease
