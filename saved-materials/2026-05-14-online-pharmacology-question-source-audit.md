# Online Pharmacology Practice Source Audit

Date: 2026-05-14

Purpose: Expand the NURS 304 practice bank with online nursing pharmacology practice-question patterns while keeping the content aligned to course study guides, lecture slide text, and saved course questions.

## Sources Consulted

- RegisteredNurseRN pharmacology/NCLEX practice pages: ACE inhibitors, beta blockers, heparin, anticoagulants, thiazide diuretics, potassium-sparing diuretics, insulin types, antiarrhythmics, aminoglycosides, tetracyclines, macrolides, and related medication-safety topics.
- Real Life Pharmacology practice pages: respiratory medications and gastrointestinal medications.
- Lakeshore Nursing Pharmacology Sample Exam Questions PDF.

## Inclusion Rules Used

- Included only topics already present in NURS 304 materials: pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics, medication safety, immune/hypersensitivity/immunosuppression, cardiovascular drugs, respiratory drugs, renal/electrolytes/diuretics, GI drugs, hematology/coagulation, endocrine drugs, and the Week 8 antibiotic classes already captured in slides.
- Adapted public online practice-question patterns into original wording rather than copying complete question banks.
- Excluded topics that were not meaningfully present in the course materials or were too advanced for the saved quiz style.
- Kept source type as `AI` because these are rewritten/adapted practice questions, not exact course questions.

## Update Performed

- Replaced 50 older AI questions that were direct, repetitive, or easier than the saved course-question style.
- Added 100 new AI/adapted questions.
- Balanced the new/adapted set across Weeks 1-8, with added emphasis on study-guide and saved-quiz themes.
- Added glossary coverage for newly introduced medication names where relevant.

## Audit Criteria

- No exact duplicate stems after insertion.
- Correct answers must appear in the answer choices for multiple-choice and select-all-that-apply questions.
- Distractors must be plausible course-level distractors rather than obviously unrelated joke answers.
- Rationales should explain why the answer is correct and briefly distinguish major distractors when useful.
- Questions should read like normal Canvas/NURS questions, avoiding artificial lead-ins.
