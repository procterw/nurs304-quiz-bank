## Medication Map

### Prednisone / Cushing Correction

The glucocorticoid card previously made it look like prednisone treated Cushing syndrome. That was misleading.

Updated behavior:

- Prednisone/glucocorticoids remain linked to Addison disease because they can replace deficient cortisol.
- Cushing syndrome is no longer listed as something glucocorticoids treat.
- Cushing-like effects are now treated as a risk of prolonged or excessive glucocorticoid exposure.

Clinical framing:

- Addison disease = too little cortisol.
- Prednisone can help replace deficient cortisol.
- Cushing syndrome = too much cortisol/glucocorticoid effect.
- Long-term prednisone can contribute to Cushing-like symptoms.
